Starter — foundational structure.

Compact · Single Service
01 / 03

Starter

Foundational Structure

A compact site that defines one primary service and a qualification path. For businesses establishing their first structured digital presence.

CapacitySingle-service architecture. Up to four governed sections.
SuitabilityOwner-led businesses with one defined service line and limited content volume.
GovernsPositioning, primary service definition, contact and qualification flow.
LayersA · Brand Foundation  ·  B · Website Architecture
ExcludedEcommerce. Multi-service hierarchy. Applied collateral.
DeliveryMilestone-based, fixed scope document.
OutcomeA structured first presence that qualifies inbound contact reliably.

Pro — expanded structure.

Multi-Service · Active Acquisition
02 / 03

Pro

Expanded Structure

A service page system with content hierarchy and basic brand application. For businesses with multiple service lines and active client acquisition.

CapacityMulti-service architecture with governed content hierarchy.
SuitabilityBusinesses with two to six defined services and recurring acquisition activity.
GovernsService page system, brand application, social media structure.
LayersA · Brand Foundation  ·  B · Architecture  ·  C · Brand Application
ExcludedEcommerce. Transactional functions.
DeliveryMilestone-based, fixed scope, staged review gates.
OutcomeA scalable service system that supports growing inbound demand.

Growth — full infrastructure.

Ecommerce · Multi-Section
03 / 03

Growth

Full Infrastructure

A multi-section architecture with ecommerce or advanced functionality and integrated brand application. For businesses scaling operations or adding transactional functions.

CapacityMulti-section site with transactional or advanced functional layer.
SuitabilityBusinesses scaling operations, adding ecommerce, or integrating multiple revenue streams.
GovernsEcommerce layer, advanced architecture, integrated brand application.
LayersA · Foundation  ·  B · Architecture  ·  C · Application  ·  D · Ecommerce
ExcludedOpen-ended creative scope. Undefined functional builds.
DeliveryMilestone-based, staged delivery, integrated review.
OutcomeA predictable infrastructure that supports operational and transactional scale.
