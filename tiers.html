Tier Logic

A tier is a capacity, not a checklist.

Most providers price by hours or features. Orbitra prices by structural capacity. The tier you select defines how much your site is built to hold — content, services, functions — and how it is governed.

This means scope is declared before delivery. Timelines are fixed. Outputs are predictable. Tier movement is explicit, not negotiated.
