01 / The System

How Orbitra works

Why tiers instead of custom projects?

Tiers govern scope. Custom projects do not. A defined tier produces a predictable timeline, a predictable price, and a predictable result. Open-ended scope produces drift — in cost, in time, and in clarity.

Is this a template marketplace?

No. Tiers define system capacity, not visual templates. Each build applies your service definition, brand inputs, and content architecture within a fixed structural envelope.

Are you an agency?

No. Orbitra is a productized website infrastructure provider. We sit between agencies and DIY builders — selling scope governance, not hours.

Who is this built for?

Service businesses with one to thirty employees, defined services, and existing revenue. Owner- or manager-led. Outcome-focused.

02 / Scope & Pricing

How scope and price are set

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is tied to tier capacity, not to hours. Each tier has a fixed price range disclosed during the scope review. There is no negotiation on rate — only on tier fit.

Can the scope be expanded mid-build?

No. Scope is locked at signature. Additional structure is delivered as a follow-on engagement or a tier upgrade. This protects timeline and result quality.

What is included in the fixed scope document?

Recommended tier, deliverables, timeline, milestones, price, and exclusions. Signed before any work begins.

Is the Tier Fit Audit free?

Yes. The audit is a structured intake. It produces a tier recommendation and a scope outline at no cost.

03 / Tier Logic

Choosing the right tier

How do I know which tier I need?

The audit determines it. Tier fit is based on service complexity, content volume, acquisition activity, and operational scale — not preference.

Can I move between tiers later?

Yes. Tier upgrades follow the same scope-governed process. Starter to Pro, or Pro to Growth, is delivered as a defined upgrade engagement.

Does Growth always include ecommerce?

Ecommerce is available at Growth only. It is not automatically included — it is scoped if the business requires transactional functionality.

04 / Exclusions

What sits outside the system

What does Orbitra not build?

No bespoke creative engagements. No experimental brand work. No personal brand or influencer sites. No undefined startup pitches. No open-ended retainers.

Do you offer ongoing creative services?

No. Engagements are milestone-based and close on delivery. Maintenance and structural additions are scoped as separate, defined engagements.

What if my request falls outside the tier system?

We decline. Clarity protects both parties. If the structure does not fit, we say so during the audit.

05 / Delivery

Process and timelines

How long does a build take?

Timeline is fixed at signature and scales with tier. Starter is the shortest. Growth is the longest. Specific durations are confirmed in the scope document.

What do you need from me?

A defined service offering, existing brand inputs if any, and content cooperation through structured milestones. The system depends on inputs arriving on schedule.

How are revisions handled?

Revisions occur at defined milestone checkpoints, governed by the signed scope. Open-ended revision cycles are not part of the model.
