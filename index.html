Ref · 02.00 — Tier System Three defined capacities

Tiers represent system capacity, not feature checklists.

Each tier is sized to a stage of operation. Selection is determined by structural fit, not preference.

Tier · 01 Starter

Foundational structure

Compact site, primary service definition, contact and qualification flow.

  • Single-service architecture
  • Defined contact path
  • Brand foundation, applied

Suitability — Businesses establishing a first structured digital presence.

Tier · 02 Pro

Expanded structure

Service page system, content hierarchy, basic brand application, social structure.

  • Multi-service architecture
  • Content hierarchy
  • Applied brand collateral

Suitability — Businesses with multiple service lines and active acquisition.

Tier · 03 Growth

Full infrastructure

Ecommerce or advanced functionality, multi-section architecture, integrated brand application.

  • Transactional functions
  • Multi-section system
  • Integrated brand layer

Suitability — Businesses scaling operations or adding transactional functions.

Ref · 04.00 — Categories Defined and excluded

What Orbitra builds. And what it does not.

A · Brand Foundation Layer

Logo system, visual rules, typography and colour structure. The base on which every applied output sits.

B · Website Architecture

The core deliverable. Sized by tier. Defines the structural capacity of the site.

C · Brand Application

Signage, social media templates, applied collateral. Available within Pro and Growth.

D · Ecommerce Layer

Available at Growth tier only. Adds transactional capacity to the system.

Out of Scope
  • Bespoke or fully custom creative engagements
  • Undefined startup branding without service structure
  • Personal brands, influencer accounts, lifestyle content
  • Open-ended retainers without defined deliverables

Ref · 06.00 — FAQ Condensed

Common questions, answered structurally.

Q · 01

Why tiers instead of custom quotes?

Tiers govern scope before work begins. They remove negotiation, prevent drift, and align cost to capacity.

Q · 02

Can a tier be modified mid-project?

No. Scope is fixed at signature. A different tier may be selected at the audit stage if structural fit changes.

Q · 03

What if a request falls outside a tier?

Out-of-scope requests are documented and either deferred or addressed as a separate, defined engagement.

Q · 04

Is pricing negotiable?

No. Each tier carries a defined price. The audit determines which tier applies.

Q · 05

Who is the system not for?

Undefined startups, experimental creative brands, personal brands, and businesses without clear service structure.
