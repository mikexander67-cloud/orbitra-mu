REF · 05.00 — NOTES

Frequently clarified.

Why tiers instead of custom quotes?+

Tiers govern scope. Custom quotes invite negotiation, scope drift, and unpredictable timelines. A defined tier removes those failure modes.

How is pricing set?+

Pricing is fixed per tier. It reflects structural capacity, not hours. The tier determines the price.

What if our needs sit between two tiers?+

The audit resolves this. We recommend the lower tier when capacity is sufficient, and the higher tier when structure requires it. We do not blend tiers.

Can scope be added later?+

Yes — by moving up a tier, not by appending to a signed scope. This keeps every engagement governed.

Who is Orbitra not for?+

Undefined startups, experimental creative brands, personal brands, and businesses without a clear service structure. The audit will say so directly.

What happens if a request falls outside the tier?+

It is logged and addressed in a subsequent tier engagement, or declined. The current scope remains protected.
