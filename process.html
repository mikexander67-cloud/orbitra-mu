The Sequence

From intake to delivery.

Each step has a defined input, a defined output, and a defined exit. Nothing advances until the prior step is closed.

01
Free · Structured Intake

Tier Fit Audit

You complete a structured intake covering your services, audience, and operational stage. We review against the tier capacity model.

Input: Intake form Output: Audit summary Duration: 48 hours
02
Recommendation · Fixed Scope

Tier Recommendation

We return a tier recommendation — Starter, Pro, or Growth — with a fixed scope document. Deliverables, sections, and category coverage are itemised.

Input: Audit summary Output: Scope document Format: Written
03
Review · Alignment

Scope Review

We walk through timeline, price, and deliverables together. Adjustments happen here — before signature, not after. This is the last point at which scope is fluid.

Input: Scope document Output: Reviewed scope Mode: Call or async
04
Signed · Locked

Signed Scope

The scope document is signed. Tier, deliverables, timeline, and price are locked. Out-of-scope requests are routed to a separate tier upgrade or addendum process.

Input: Reviewed scope Output: Signed agreement Status: Locked
05
Milestone-Based · Predictable

Milestone Delivery

Work proceeds through defined milestones tied to the signed scope. Each milestone has a deliverable and a review checkpoint. No phase runs open-ended.

Input: Signed scope Output: Live system Cadence: Milestone gated
Operating Principles

What governs the process.

Four principles structure every engagement. They are not preferences — they are the system.

Scope before work

No build begins until scope is signed. Discovery and definition are separated from delivery.

Fixed timelines

Each tier carries a defined delivery window. Milestones are gated, not estimated.

Defined inputs

Each step requires a specific input. Ambiguity is resolved upstream, never carried forward.

Routed exceptions

Out-of-scope requests route to a tier upgrade or addendum — not silent expansion.

Conventional Custom Project

  • Open-ended discovery phase
  • Scope expands during build
  • Timeline estimated, not gated
  • Pricing negotiated mid-project

Orbitra Process

  • Structured audit with defined output
  • Scope locked before signature
  • Milestone-gated delivery
  • Tier-based fixed pricing
